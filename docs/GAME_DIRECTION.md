# PRIME KINGDOMS game direction

Build a civilization, command armies, form alliances, conquer territory and grow
from Village through Town, City, Country and Kingdom into Empire.

The player controls the kingdom through an independent strategy camera, building
selection, court decisions and strategic army orders. The ruler is a visual symbol,
never a walked or steered avatar. Direct movement, joystick, jump, sword, crouch
and horse control are removed from the production architecture.

Resource production feeds construction, research, training, treatment and diplomacy.
Armies and commanders contest contiguous holdings. Authoritative reports, conquest,
prestige, objectives and clan wars feed realm and player progression. Cosmetic
heraldry has no effect on fundamental soldier quality.

Art direction is serious medieval realism, credible architecture and adult human
proportions. The reference is a quality target, not permission to copy another
game's assets, names, UI, numbers or identity. Current assets and systems must
continue improving until the final quality gates are met.
